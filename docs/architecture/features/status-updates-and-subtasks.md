# Status updates and subtasks

**Status updates** (cr27, schema v72): a task's dated lines of text — what happened, as of a day — in their own table (`statusUpdates`, `status-update-repository` → `status-update-store`; not on the task row, but each write — add, edit, remove — stamps the task's `modifiedAt` in the same transaction, so updates keep a task from going stale, and its undo / redo puts the earlier / its own stamp back), newest first by day, then by when written. They replaced the one-line Progress field (the v72 upgrade made each task's Progress its first entry, dated its `modifiedAt`); the Status picker (`statusId`) is a separate field. Added / edited / removed in the task layout's Status updates section — the editor's and the review card's — in **the right-hand column** (task layout B, feedback 2026-11-15: beside Subtasks and Notes, under the fact band; its heading at the column's top, the log and the add line **anchored to its bottom**, straight above the host's footer, so the line typed into sits at the foot of the card; held short — the editor at its viewport cap, the review card in its cell — the log scrolls inside down to one entry, the add line never shrinking; under 600px of body the layout is one column — Subtasks, Notes, Status updates — and the body scrolls as one; its **heading always shows**, the count beside it once there is an entry; the follow-up fields are the footer's, `TaskFollowupFields`: `Last [date] · Next [date] [1d][1w][1m][3m]` (Settings › Tasks' quick follow-up intervals) just before Mark followed up, their help in the footer's spare room, the quick intervals giving way first as the footer narrows, then the fields' relative words, then the labels): **every entry oldest first**, like a log, the list scrolling inside and opening at the bottom, then the **add line**: ⏎ adds the entry dated today with `addStatusUpdate`'s rules — in a review card, ⏎ there is Mark followed up with that update; a past day is set by editing the entry — each its own undo step; the Review widget's medium face shows the heading, the latest two entries (read-only), the update line and the Next row; the places that show only the newest (the row's tooltip, the phone chip, the check-in message, the follow-up popover) stay newest; added from the notes popover's Status line (today) and from the follow-up popover's optional **Status update** line — with text, Mark followed up adds the entry dated today (`followedUp`) in the same undo step (`services/mark-followed-up`; from the bulk bar, to every selected task).

**Entering one on an open task counts as following up on its day** (`addStatusUpdate`): a day after the task's last follow-up is Mark followed up for that day — the last follow-up moves to it, the next follow-up by the rule counted from it, entry `followedUp`, one undo step with the snackbar; an older or same day only adds the entry (entries on one task are decided one after another, each after the last one's follow-up has landed, however fast they come — `inTaskOrder`), and so does any entry on a **completed** task (nothing is chased on it any more: its follow-up dates stay, the entry isn't `followedUp`); a new task's entries set its Last follow-up to the newest's day (a new task is always created open). The follow-up popover's Mark followed up (and the bulk bar's, and a check-in reply's Record a follow-up) is the user's explicit choice and records the follow-up on a completed task too. Editing an entry's day doesn't, nor does a check-in reply without Record a follow-up. The newest shows on the row's notes tooltip (the phone row shows a notes mark only); the text search reads every entry. A task delete takes its entries, and its undo brings them back; Duplicate copies them. They travel with exports, backups, the sync file, task archives and the append import; the audit deletes orphans

**Subtasks** (cr28, schema v73): a task's checklist — each line a title and whether it is done, nothing else, one level — in its own table (`subtasks`, `subtask-repository` → `subtask-store`, ordered by `sortOrder`). A subtask is never a list row, never counted, filtered, grouped or sorted, and never a search result of its own; **the text search finds the task by its subtasks' words** (a Subtasks group in the top bar, the line as the snippet; the screen filter, lists and counts keep the task likewise). Every task row shows a `2/5` count after its title — a 12px pie before it filling by the share done, the accent once all are (D25 D2) — that opens the checklist as a popover (check, rename, add, ⋯: Rename · Make a task · Move to another task… · Delete); an open task without one shows a faint checklist mark there while the row is hovered or focused (`SubtaskAddButton`, Rows and Stacked; always on touch), which opens the empty checklist on its add line; the phone row shows the count on its details line, and its task menu's Add subtask… opens the checklist as a bottom sheet; the task layout's writing has a Subtasks section, first, above Notes (task layout B; its heading always shown; drag to reorder; **+ Add subtask** its last line; the checkboxes and that line's plus start at the heading's left edge, a row's grip hanging in the gutter) — the editor's in create mode too, written with the task, and the review card's. The task menu offers **Add subtask…** and **Make subtask of…** (a task picker; the task keeps only its title and done state — a prompt lists what goes unless it holds only its title — its own subtasks move to the parent; one undo step brings it back whole; `services/subtask-convert`); **Make a task** gives a subtask the parent's people, tags, status, due date, next follow-up and markers (not its notes, status updates or repeat). Every subtask write stamps the task's `modifiedAt` as status updates do (`stores/task-hooks`); checking the last open one offers **Complete task** in the undo snackbar (never automatic); completing a task with open subtasks asks nothing; a recurring completion unchecks the checklist in the same undo step; a delete takes the checklist and its undo brings it back; Duplicate copies it unchecked; purge removes it. Notes → tasks (the toolbar's Convert, Alt+T, or the convert icon in the editor's left margin beside the line under the pointer; one line opens Quick Add on its text, Details handing off to the editor; two or more open a preview that shows drafts on task rows edited in place, nothing stored until it is confirmed; a draft checked off makes no task and its line goes on confirm, in the one note edit that a Ctrl+Z undoes; Settings › Tasks › New tasks' Remove note lines after converting (off by default) makes every converted line go that way too, asking nothing, instead of `✓ <title>`; × leaves the line) makes the indented list lines under a bullet its task's subtasks (plain titles), and Tab / ⇧Tab — or the toolbar's Indent / Outdent — indent list lines in every notes editor.

**A task's own notes** (the editor's, the notes popover's) convert to that task's subtasks instead, the same three ways and with no dialog (`services/note-subtasks`): each line a plain title, the lines under it beside it (one level), a done box done; the lines turn `✓` or go as above, all one undo step with the snackbar; in create mode they join the pending subtasks. Check-in messages list a task's open subtasks under it for context (never read back); the Markdown export and Copy write indented `- [ ]` / `- [x]` lines under the task. It travels with exports, backups, the sync file, task archives and the append import; the audit deletes orphans
