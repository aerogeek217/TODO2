# Widget chrome: tabs, faces and Open larger

**Name and icon…** (items 11, 14; the widget part of the ⋯ / right-click menu, before Change widget ›; desktop): a popover under the tab (or its top line) — Name (a list's is its list's, written as Edit list writes it; a Number's, a chart's and Created vs completed's their `config.name`; any other kind's the tab's optional `name`, blank = the kind's label) and Icon (the shared `IconPicker`) —, each change one layout write and one undo step (`Renamed tab to “Inbox”`, `Changed icon of “Inbox”`). A tab's optional `icon` (`models/icon`: none, a line icon, an emoji muted or in color; no schema step) shows on the strip, the `+N` menu, the drag ghost, the widget popup's header and the phone; a tab never given one shows its kind's glyph there (J1), and alone in its cell a picked icon leads its top line (J2). A muted emoji is one color like a line icon — gray at rest, the accent on a cell's active tab —, a color emoji keeps its colors.

**Tabs on demand** (D26 AA1): a cell holding one widget has no tab strip — the widget's own top line (`WidgetTopLine`) names it and stands for its tab (`data-ws-tab`): a list's name and badge capsule (`list/ListTopLine`) then its filter line (the filter in words, the count while narrowed, group · sort), the calendar's month header, a Number's label, a chart's title, a name line for Notes and the Neglect map; it ends with the cell's controls slot (`CellControls`, lent through `CellControlsContext`: **Open larger** where the widget offers it (below), and the ⋯ — shown on the cell's hover and focus-within and always on touch, with no fade; floating over a face, hidden, they take no press — a tap or a press at a Number's or small face's corner reaches the face; while collapsed an Expand row button that always shows); a face with no top line (a small face, a Number whose label is inside its button) gets the slot floating over the cell's top-right corner. With two or more widgets the cell's header shows the tab strip (`CellTabStrip`), its empty space (only room; in the mode a press there reaches the card's surface), then Open larger (where the active widget offers it), Collapse row and the same ⋯ (in the mode the card's ⋯ stands for these); on a device that hovers, the active tab's × and the add-tab `+` show only on the cell's hover or focus-within (opacity, so the strip's fit doesn't change; never hidden on touch). A drop joining a one-widget cell previews the strip it will have.

**A strip of Numbers** (flair P10, `workspace-rows.isNumberStripCell`, `NumberStrip`): a cell of two or more tabs, every one a Number, draws no tab strip but its Numbers side by side, evenly divided (`repeat(auto-fit, minmax(72px, 1fr))`, wrapping onto another line when each can't keep 72px, the fit row growing with it) — each a slot of its own (`[data-number-slot]`) with its own face, its own ⋯ and Open larger (its controls on its own hover, `data-slot-hot`), its × and a tab's drag (reorder in the strip; dragged out, and in from another card, only in the mode); its right edge is a gutter for the `+` that adds a Number, clear of every Number; each Number's ⋯ has Remove widget (that Number alone); a drop in the strip's middle lands between its slots (`measure` reads the slots as its tabs, `Add to strip`), its sides split (in the mode). On a phone its Numbers are two-up items among the others. Schema v82 made every run of side-by-side single-Number cells in a row a strip.

**Fit rows**: a row track whose cells lying only in it each hold only Numbers (one, or a strip) fits its content (`auto`, 58px for a Number of any shape measured; derived, never stored — `workspace-rows.isFitTrack`; a cell spanning through it doesn't count) — unless its divider gave it a height (the track's `fitPx`, view state saved at once; it never replaces the track's weight or px, a split's halves don't inherit it, and while the row isn't Numbers-only it is ignored, the track weighted by its own weight; a double-click on the divider clears it; a Number taller than its small face shows its medium face; collapsed, the row's cells keep their small face).

**Faces** (D25 R, `utils/widget-face` + `hooks/use-widget-face`): a widget draws its small, medium or large face by its cell body's measured size — small at most 90px tall or 180px wide, medium at most 160px tall, else large; never stored, never a setting. The List, Calendar, Neglect map, Review (its own thresholds, item 13), the Planner (its own, item 14) and every glance widget have faces (Notes is always whole; a fit row's cell is at its small face); a click on a small face (or its focus-only `Open …` button) opens the widget larger.

**Open larger** (the widget popup, `components/workspace/WidgetPopup` hosted once on the workspace page, its state `ui-store.widgetPopup`, opened through `services/widget-popup`): one widget at a time drawn at its large face over the workspace — `min(1400px, 100vw − 48px)` × `100vh − 48px`, centered, on the dialogs' overlay tier (`DialogFrame`; the full screen on a phone, where the popup review shows the Review widget's medium face) —, the same tab as the cell's, not a copy (its filter, sort, zoom and edits are the cell's); **a tab has one live widget at a time**: while the popup shows a tab, its cell (desktop and phone) draws a quiet placeholder in the widget's place (`OpenLargerPlaceholder`: `“X” is open larger`, muted, under a name line alone in its cell; it keeps the body's height where its content sets it) and mounts the widget again when the popup closes — two live copies would share their drag ids in the page's one `DndContext`; its header the tab's icon, its title (the popup's accessible name), its own ⋯ (`Widget options`: the widget's entries only — no cell layout entries, no Open larger, no Name and icon…, no Change widget ›) and ×; what opens from inside (menus, the editors — anchored in the popup while it shows their tab, `utils/dom-contract.widgetElement` —, the task editor, the task dialog) is a layer above it. Ways in: the Open larger button — in a one-widget cell's controls slot before the ⋯, and in a tab strip's header (the active tab's widget; shown with the header's other buttons; none in a header under 340px wide, where the strip needs that room for its tabs), Open larger in the ⋯ menu's widget section (before Name and icon…), the palette's **Open Widget Larger** (the cell last used, as Add Widget › picks it), a small face's click and a face's `+N more`. Esc, × or a press on the scrim close it, the focus back on what opened it (the tab's cell when that is gone); it closes when its tab goes (a layout switch, the tab closed) and on a data replace. Opening it is asked about first by an open editor with changes, as opening Settings is (an unchanged one closes); opening another replaces it.

**Numbers** offer it only where their large face draws more than the face their cell shows (`utils/widget-face.tabOpensLarger`): a count with a breakdown (only the large face draws it), and Share of…, Oldest, Against a goal and a group from their small face (which leaves out the ring and `N of M`, the caption, title and foot, the state and bar, the item names); a plain count never — its click opens its tasks, as ever —, nor a Number that follows a list, whose click opens that list in the popup (`openNumberList`: a list widget under the Number's tab id, so its drag ids differ from the list's own tab's; its zoom and details width are the popup's own, kept while the workspace is open, never saved). Stored layouts are read through the layout repair (`data/layout-shape`: a part this build can't use is dropped, a tiling that doesn't hold is rebuilt as the cells in their reading order stacked in one column; the repair stays in memory, with a notice, until the layout's first edit writes it — view state isn't written before that). A layout's optional **hue** (D19) colours its tab, its cells' top lines and headers and the focus ring inside it; it is set with the name in **Name, icon and hue…** (the layout tab's menu and the picker; the dialog of that name) and saved at once. So is its optional **icon** (widget feedback 11, 15, J1; `WorkspaceLayout.icon`, an `IconValue`; the dialog's Icon row between Name and Hue, the shared icon picker): absent, the layout glyph as before; No icon, none; drawn before the name on its tab, in the picker and on the phone — a line icon and a muted emoji gray at rest and in the hue on the open tab, a color emoji in its own colors. Statuses take an emoji the same way (J17: the status editor's picker, its Icons tab the status glyphs; `Status.emoji` / `emojiShow` beside the glyph's `icon`, drawn by `shared/StatusMark` wherever a status is). The picker's Recent is every emoji on tabs, layouts and statuses (`hooks/use-recent-emoji`).
