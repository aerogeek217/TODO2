# Ambience

**Ambience** (desktop only; Settings › Appearance › Ambience — on by default): quiet signs of the time of day and the season in the chrome, never over a task's text and never in a colour that means something. `hooks/use-ambience` computes the `utils/sky` scene once a minute from the clock and the place (a typed latitude / longitude, else the time zone's main city — `utils/sun`; no location prompt, no network, so the offline file stays clean). `components/ambience` draws it: in the top bar, the sky over the glass at the share the glass lets through, the sun and moon as soft discs, stars (varied sizes, a few bright, a slow shallow twinkle), on some nights a steady evening planet, cumulus, the winter aurora, morning mist, the season's weather (snow, leaves, petals, seeds, fireflies — CSS-animated), the rare sky events — a shooting star every 2–5 minutes and a UFO every 1–2 hours in the dark sky, an airplane every 5–10 minutes by day (`utils/sky-events` + `hooks/use-sky-events`: timers, the motion CSS; none under reduced motion or at Header motion 0) — and the sun's point on the hairline; in the workspace, the sunbeam behind the cells (no falling weather there: moving sprites behind the tasks distract) and lamplight over them; a task completed on screen sends a ripple along the hairline and a burst from its box. The daytime things fade out through dusk — gone by full night: the clouds, and the leaves, petals and seeds (snow keeps falling; fireflies come out). Sky strength scales the sky, clouds, aurora, mist, sunbeam, stars, planet and sky events; Header motion everything that moves in the bar; Seasons the weather, the burst and the aurora. Reduced motion keeps a still sky. Its colours are `--ambience-*` tokens in `tokens.css` (each theme's own there), which the scene mixes by the time of day with `color-mix()` — no ambience file writes a colour or picks one by theme. One JSON settings row (`ambience`), no schema step
