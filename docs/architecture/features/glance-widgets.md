# Glance widgets

**Glance widgets** (D25 N, D26; `components/workspace/glance/`): the dashboard's pieces, each a widget kind of its own composed in the layout's table. Each reads the screen filter as it is (`stores/screen-filter`), and the stats base (`useStatsBase`), the calendar and the Neglect map with it. The add-widget and Change widget menus list them under **Insights**, after the **Tasks** kinds (List, Calendar, Review, Planner, Notes): Number, Breakdown (adding it opens its presets — People · Tags · Status · Custom), Pivot and Created vs completed, then the Neglect map, Work clusters and the List wall — whole widgets, in the group for the menus only (`WidgetKindSpec.group` is read by the menus alone; the glance minimums, fit rows and the right-click rule read `glance` / `fitHeight`, which the Calendar and the Neglect map don't have, so moving them between groups changed nothing but the menus).

**v78** (`upgrades/v78/widget-set`, D26 P4) removed Needs follow-up, Next 7 days, Stale tasks, Recently completed, What changed and Today — their saved tabs dropped, a cell they leave without tabs closed by the absorb rule (item 4); **Clear Focus…** went with Today — and made the Eisenhower summary the Eisenhower's small face (its tab an Eisenhower tab, keeping its id); the Eisenhower itself was removed after v78 (item 6).

**A Number is a filter** (D26 P5, `models/glance-widgets.NumberConfig` `{ name, color?, unlitColor?, filter, show, litWhen, litAt?, compare, breakdown? }`): a name, two colours — **when lit** (`color`; absent = Auto, the accent) and **otherwise** (`unlitColor`, widget feedback 2D, optional, no schema step; absent = Auto: the name muted, the count in the text colour, a 0 muted, the line in the accent) —, its name, count and 14-day line in whichever applies (a 0 in a picked colour otherwise dimmed, `number-colors.dimmed`; a shape lit by its own rule — Oldest, Against a goal — the same; a group's items keep their one colour, lit by the group's rule) (each drawn per theme with nothing stored changed — `utils/theme-color`: a preset as its hue's text token, v78's four metric colours as their meanings' tokens, any other hex shaded by `--custom-color-shade`, 0% in dark), a Graphical / Expression filter — no presets, no metric list: a new Number's filter is empty and counts every open task; a Completed range admits the completed tasks in it —, when it lights (`litWhen` never / above / below `litAt`, D18), and what its change compares with (yesterday, the same day last week, none). It counts its filter's tasks among those the top bar shows, as a list does (`stores/screen-filter`) — as `shown of total` (`services/stats/number-count`); a click opens them in the task dialog (titled by its name, its filter in words, `glance-drills.numberDrill`). Its editor (`NumberEditor`, board X: name, **Color when lit** and **Color otherwise** — each a `ColorPicker` with Auto before its swatches; with Light up when Never one **Color**, the colour otherwise, the lit one kept as stored, the filter with the Graphical / Expression switch and Reads as, the shape and its fields, Light up when, Compare with) opens from **Edit number…** in the ⋯, beside the Number's body (adding a Number opens nothing: a new one counts every open task); it is hosted on `WorkspacePage` outside the widget tree (`NumberEditorHost`, `ui-store.numberEditorTabId`). **Every widget editor — the Number's, a chart's, Created vs completed's — is Edit list's Save / Discard** (`glance/editor-draft.useEditorDraft`, its foot `EditorFooter`): its controls edit a copy of the config, which the widget draws live (`stores/widget-preview-store`, read by `GlanceWidget`); **Save** (off while nothing changed or while a filter expression doesn't read — the fields report through `shared/filters/expression-report`) writes only the keys that changed, for good, as one layout config update — one undo step (a ⋯ menu's write meanwhile, e.g. Tags, stays), and **Discard** drops the copy. Esc, × or a press elsewhere close it at once while nothing changed, else ask Save / Discard / Keep editing (the shared confirm prompt, `Save changes to number “Open”?`; the press that asked goes no further). Nothing is kept across a reload.

**Its figures roll** (flair P1, `shared/RollingNumber`): while mounted, each digit of a count or a shape's main figure that changes slides out as the new one slides in — up as it rises, down as it falls, about 280 ms, nothing on first render, the text the plain figure —, and a figure that stops being lit cross-fades to its unlit colour and sends one ring in its lit colour out from it (`glance/LightRing`; nothing on becoming lit, none under reduced motion). Its small and medium faces are its name over its count, with its 14-day line and its change beside the count **wherever they fit** (widget feedback 2D: decided by the room, not the face, so a Numbers row shows them too — the count, line and change are one line box of the count's height that clips what wraps, the change dropping out first, then the line, never the count; CSS only, so nothing flickers on a divider drag, and the row is no taller — the line needs about 115px of cell beside a one-digit count, the change some 220–280px by its words; a Share of… likewise, its line and change under its percent); large draws it bigger with a breakdown by person, tag, status (each status in Settings' order with its glyph, then No status) or priority (the Priority sort's groups — Focus, Urgent + Important, Urgent, Important — with their rings, then No priority), each line opening its tasks.

**Number shapes** (D26 P6, `models/number-shapes`, `show`; the config holds only the active shape's field, `use-number-shapes` + `services/stats/number-shapes`): **Count**; **Share of…** (its tasks as a share of a second filter's, `of` — every open task by default —: a ring, the percent and `N of M`, a line of percents); **Oldest** (the age in days of its stalest task by last follow-up — never followed up: since created —, created, modified or due, that task's title and `N · median D days` (N the tasks that have that date: the ones its click opens); lit by the age); **Against a goal** (at most / at least N, optionally a week with its pace as a tick; lit while behind pace — the first day of the week is compared with 0, so a week never opens lit — or over the limit); a **group** of up to six counts in one cell, each its own name, filter and colour, each opening its tasks; **Follow a list** (one of the layout's lists as it shows — its filter as edited, its prompt and the screen filter — with its tab badge; a click opens the list in the widget popup; the layout counts that list as shown, `utils/workspace-layout-lists`, so it is kept and Duplicate layout repoints it). Only Count and Share of… compare.

**v78** (`upgrades/v78/numbers`, the map frozen in `number-metrics`) made each saved `{ metric }` the Graphical filter that counts the same tasks — Open none, Overdue `overdue`, Due today `due today`, Due in the next 7 days `due within 6 days` (today … today + 6), Follow-up due `followup <= today`, No follow-up date `no followup`, Focus `/focus`, Urgent `/urgent`, Closed this week `completed this week` (compared with last week) —, named by its old label, its colour the metric's as a hex (the accent's metrics Auto), lit above 0 where it lit before.

**Charts** (D26 P8, board Z1, `models/chart-widgets`): each a name and a filter whose tasks it splits (among those the top bar shows, as a Number counts), with an editor on the Number editor's pattern (`ChartEditor`: name, filter, and the kind's own fields; **Edit breakdown… / pivot…** in the ⋯ (never on add); hosted on the page as `ChartEditorHost`, `chart-widgets-store.editorTabId`); every bar, cell and segment opens its tasks in the task dialog (`chart-drills`, titled `<chart> · <row>`); small face its title and top entry, medium and large the chart.

**Breakdown** (`breakdown`, `services/stats/breakdown`): split by people, tags, status, priority (the Priority sort's exclusive groups, each with its ring), due (Overdue · Today · Next 7 days · Later; the first reads Before today once a completed task is among them) or age (since created), drawn as bars, a donut or one stacked bar, showing each row's count or its oldest task's age; a table of name · Open (Tasks while its filter lets completed tasks through, as its header and a Pivot say; or Oldest) — built in — then its **count columns** (G3, board BC; every other column is one — the fixed Priority column went, a sort stored by it reading as the default order; `BreakdownConfig.columns`, up to four, each `{ id, name, color, filter, litWhen, litAt?, age? }`: a row's cell counts that row's tasks the column's Graphical / Expression filter keeps, as a Number counts — filled solid in the column's colour while the count meets its Light up when rule, outlined in it otherwise (outlined, the colour as the theme draws it as text, `utils/theme-color`), a dim `–` for none; a click jumps to the Review widget with those tasks as its filter (item 13; with no Review widget anywhere, it opens them in the task dialog); each column 40px, its number's room — its heading cut with an ellipsis, its name in the tooltip — the name column taking the rest; set in the editor's Count columns — name, colour, Light up when, filter, ×, + Add column. A column may carry the row's **follow-up age** after its count (board V1, option B; `age: { yellowAfterDays }`, optional, no schema step — the layout repair checks it): `[3] 23d`, the oldest of the row's open tasks by last follow-up (never followed up: since created; a completed task never counts), muted, in the follow-up color past its threshold, a dim `–` with no open task, the cell 100px; its tooltip names that task and the median; a click jumps as the row's Review does; the heading's small `age` toggle sorts by it (`age:<columnId>`, oldest first, view state); Copy writes `Follow-up 3 · 23d`; the editor's Days since follow-up after the count and Yellow past [14] days; a new People Breakdown's (and Change widget's) Follow-up column has it. A new Breakdown (every preset, and Change widget) writes two as its `columns` (`defaultBreakdownColumns`, in `workspace-store`'s seed): **Due** (`due` by today + N, overdue included) and **Follow-up** (`followup` likewise), N Settings › Tasks' ranges of the moment, each lit above 0 in its colour — ordinary columns from then on.

**Schema v79** wrote them on every stored config without `columns`, from its old `badges` and the data's own Settings ranges (a range `off` dropping its column, `overdue` counting only the late tasks), dropped `badges`, and moved a sort stored by the older derived columns (`overdue`, `due-soon` → `due`; `followup-late`, `followup-due` → `followup`) to its column (one by the old Priority column, or by a column it doesn't have, removed); a config without `columns` (a hand-made file) reads the two defaults in Settings' ranges (`models/chart-widgets.breakdownColumns`); the ⋯'s Due › / Follow-up › range submenus went) · the row's action — **Review**, a muted 24px icon button right after the row's name, in the name column (no column of its own; the name ellipsizes before it), shown while the row is hovered or holds the focus (opacity only, so nothing shifts; always shown without hover), hidden while no layout has a Review widget (the person row's envelope went with decision 7; v79 removed its stored `checkins`) —, each heading a sort (counts largest first, names A–Z, a second click reversing it, a third back to the default: most counted across the count columns first for people and tags, the natural order for status and priority; the none row — Unassigned, No tags, No status, No priority — last; per split, view state saved at once) — no filter field of its own (its filter row went: the top bar's `@` / `#` picks narrow its rows, below); the medium face a compact name · count columns table; the header has no sort words (the heading's ▼ / ▲ says it). Its people and tags rows narrow by the top bar's `@` / `#` picks as a list grouped by them does (`build-list.groupRestrictions`, `services/stats/row-restrictions`); a person's or tag's right-click opens the one person / tag menu (`EntityMenu`). On a row, its Review button and a count column's count jump to the Review widget with those tasks as its filter (item 13). Presets (`breakdownPreset`): People, Tags and Status (every open task by theirs) and Custom (Waiting on went with count columns, G3: a People Breakdown with the column `!Waiting`).

**v78** (`upgrades/v78/breakdown`) made each saved People, Tags and Status tab a Breakdown with the same id (bars, every open task, Unassigned / No tag shown as they were).

**Pivot** (`pivot`, `services/stats/pivot`): rows × columns, each a Breakdown split, as a heat table with totals, a cell's colour its share of the largest; **its headings sort the rows** (item 8, as a Breakdown's: the name heading — what the rows split by, `Person` —, each column and Total are buttons, a click sorting largest first (names A–Z), a second reversing, a third back to the natural order, ▼ / ▲ on the sorted one; the none row last; the columns never move; `PivotViewState.sort`, view state saved at once, no undo step, no schema step — a sort by a column the table no longer shows reads as the natural order, `sortPivotRows`). A **Status flow** chart and the **Tag map** shipped in v78 and were then removed: v79 dropped every saved one (a cell it empties closing).

**Created vs completed** (`trend`, widget feedback 2C) is a name (default `Created vs completed`), a Graphical / Expression filter (absent: every task) counted among the tasks the top bar shows, a **Show as** and a Range (14 days, daily; 4 / 12 weeks, weekly): **Bars** (created and completed per bar, the totals and the net change), **Burndown** (the open tasks it counts at each day's end, the start, now and the change; a falling or rising line) or **Cumulative** (running totals: created, starting on the tasks open when the range began, and completed), drawn as built SVG nodes with a transparent button per bar or point (a day, a week on the weekly ranges) opening its tasks (`glance-drills`: `trendBucketDrill`, `trendOpenDrill`, `trendCumulativeDrill`); its small face is its name and the presentation's headline (the net, the open count now, the completed count), Copy writes the presentation's lines (`glance-summary.trendLines`). While neither its filter nor the screen filter narrows, every task is read as it is (`services/stats/trend.computeTrend`); else each task is replayed to each day's end (`computeTrend`'s `TrendFilter`, over the fields window `fields-<range>`, every event type a filter's history reads) and counted while the filter kept it then — a tag added mid-range brings a task in from that day —, and the days the history log can't count exactly (`filter-history.historyExactFrom`: before `historyLogSince` for the H3 fields, always for text and the modified date) are dimmed bars / dashed lines, their tooltip saying which fields read as they are now (`glance-copy.approximateNote`) — no ≈ note on the chart, as a Number's line has none; Copy names such a bar `Thu 8 (approximate)`. Its config fields `name`, `filter`, `show` are optional: v79 wrote `name` and `show` on every stored tab and a new tab writes them; a tab without them (a name renamed blank, a hand-made file) reads its default name, every task, Bars (`models/glance-widgets.trendName` / `trendShow`, `hooks/use-glance-data.trendFilter`).

**Edit chart…** in its ⋯ (before Range) opens its editor under its title (`TrendEditor`, hosted on the page as `TrendEditorHost`, `chart-widgets-store.trendEditorTabId`; Save / Discard as the Number editor's, undo step `Edited chart “X”`): name, filter with Reads as, Show as, Range. Adding it opens no editor. No glance widget shows task rows; every glance widget's ⋯ ends with **Copy** (its lines). Every statistic is a pure `services/stats/*` function over the screen-filtered tasks and over all tasks (`hooks/use-glance-data`: a hook per part over one shared base, each part a `utils/shared-memo.sharedMemo` keyed by its inputs' identity — a Number's and a chart's by their filter, `sharedMemoByKey` —, so a dozen widgets on screen — the calendar and the Neglect map among them (`useScreenTasks`) — filter the tasks once and compute each statistic once; every Number's line counts from one shared replay of the tasks, and after a write it is drawn a moment later, off the write's render; a write that leaves the shown tasks as they were recomputes none; `hooks/use-number-shapes` and `hooks/use-chart-data` build on the same base).

**History** (H3, D26 P7): besides `created` / `completed` / `reopened`, due-date and status changes, `todoEvents` now log the next follow-up (`followup`), the last follow-up (`followedUp`), the priority flags (`marker`) and each person and tag added or removed (`person` / `tag`) — at the repository's diff, the assignment joins (the bulk bar, the editor, the pickers, check-in replies, Make a task, Duplicate, the append import, a create's people and tags, deleting a person or tag — Find unused…'s removal too —) and each undo / redo —, and a sync-neutral `historyLogSince` settings row is stamped once at the first launch on v78 (and after a data replace that brings none); an append import moves it forward when the file's log doesn't reach back as far as the browser's — a file with no stamp (a task archive, an older file) to now, a later stamp to that stamp — so a past day before it reads ≈ rather than exact (`data/append-import.coverAppendedHistory`). A filter's history (`services/stats/filter-history`) replays every task to each day's end (`task-history.taskFieldReplay` → `taskAsOf`) and evaluates the filter with that day as today; a field on a day before the log began reads as it was when the log began, so a line that reads one there — or text, or the modified date, never logged — is approximate: the line's tooltip (`NumberLine`) names the fields (`historyExactness`) and its accessible name says "About" — no ≈ mark beside it (widget feedback 2D: a screen filter naming people, tags or priorities made a lone ≈ under the line). The trend and a Number's line (its filter under its screen filter, 14 days) read the events through `trend-events-store` (a window each: a trend range, a trend range's fields window `fields-14d` …, `history`). A count's change is against yesterday or the same day last week.

**v77** converted every saved Dashboard tab into the glance widgets of its parts (a tile a Number, a list tile a List cell, each section its kind, in rows of cells), which v78 then carried into the table. The **Neglect map** (D25 board H, widget kind `neglect`) plots every open task the screen filter keeps by days untouched (`modifiedAt`, capped at 60) against days until due (14 late … 30 ahead), the pure placement in `utils/neglect-map`: due soon (Settings' due-soon range) or late and untouched for the tab's threshold (default 14 days) is **Slipping** — the shaded corner — the other corners In hand / Planned / Drifting, and a strip under it holds the tasks without a due date (**Forgotten** once untouched).
