# Review

**Review** (D26, board BL; widget feedback 2A / 2B): there is no review dialog: a review happens in **the Review widget** (below), and **every Review button jumps to it** (`services/review-jump.jumpToReview`): **a Review widget on the open layout** — its first in reading order — has its filter set (`services/review-widget.setReviewWidgetFilter`: temporary, `list-widget-store.reviewFilters`), its tab shown (`showTab`: its cell's active tab, its rows expanded) and the focus moved into it (the card's update line, else its field); an open editor with changes asks Save / Discard / Keep editing first only when showing the widget would hide another tab of its cell or close the widget popup (Keep editing cancels the jump), and the widget popup closes unless it shows that very widget.

**Otherwise the popup review**: a Review widget in the widget popup (item 4's Open larger) tied to no layout tab (`REVIEW_POPUP_TAB_ID`, `services/widget-popup.openReviewPopup`), opened with that filter — another layout is never switched to; an editor with changes asks first, as the popup asks. Either way the task dialog and the filtered-tasks panel close. A jump replaces whatever the widget was reviewing, without asking — a different filter starts the review over (the first card, no marks), the same filter sent again resumes where it stood — and **saves nothing**: no layout write, no undo step.

**The buttons always show**, the phone's too (only a dialog the task dialog can't show above leaves them out, `useEntityMenuOffers`). Each sends a filter built as a tree of ids, never as text (`services/review`): a list tab's ⋯ menu › **Review these N tasks**, the editable `R` shortcut and the palette's **Review List** — the list's whole filter as it shows (its chips or expression as one expression, `wholeExpression`, its prompt pick taking that field's place as the list applies it, `applyRuntimeFilter`; a list that narrows by nothing sends an `and` of nothing, every open task; its manual order and group dividers don't carry over, J5; N the open tasks it shows); a person's or a tag's right-click menu **Review with X** / **Review #tag tasks**, the person task dialog's **Review** and the palette's **Review With ›** — `@X` / `#tag`; the top bar's People filter — `@A or @B`; a Breakdown row's **Review** icon — the Breakdown's own filter AND the row (`breakdownRowTerm`: a person, tag or status — a people or tags row under picks on both the Breakdown and the top bar also `not` the ones every pick names, so the review keeps what the row counts; a priority group as the Priority sort's exclusive groups — `/urgent and /important and not /focus`, …; a due or age bucket as a day-offset range of the due or created date; the none row Unassigned / No tags / No status / No priority / No due date), and a count column's count — AND that column's filter (a row no filter says falls back to the task dialog; every split's rows are expressible; a Breakdown whose filter lets completed tasks through — Show completed, a Completed range — opens its rows' and counts' tasks in the task dialog too, a review taking open tasks only: `breakdownReviews`); the filtered-tasks panel's **Review these N tasks** — the top bar's chips and its text search as one expression. The review's parts (`components/review`, shared by the widget's faces): the rail (`ReviewRail`) has a **sort** at its top (item 7: `Sort: Priority ▾` by default — Queue order, Due, Next follow-up, Last follow-up, Priority, Title; `services/review-queue.reviewOrder`, the keys a list sorts by, ties in queue order): the order is taken when the sort is picked (it doesn't move while the review edits tasks; a task that joins a live queue takes its sorted place among the rest, which stay where they are — `review-queue.joinOrder`), Back / Skip step in it, the current card stays; ← / → on a row are Back / Skip. Its rows are **two lines** (board BL; the rail 300px, giving way to 220px in a small cell; **under 820px wide it folds away** and a **Queue** button opens it as a popover — `ReviewQueueButton`, the rail with its sort; a pick or Esc closes it; task layout A): the mark, the box with the priority ring — **a checkbox** in the widget's rail (beside the row's button, never inside it; a tab stop after its row): it completes or reopens the task where it is, with the store's undo, without picking the row; a completed task leaves the live queue, the current one staying, checked, until the review moves on; the focus goes to the checkbox of the row that takes its place — and the whole title, then under the title the next follow-up after a small flag and the due date after a small clock, in words and the rows' colours; the current row has the accent's tint and a 2px accent edge; **✓ on every task followed up today** (item 11: its last follow-up is today, in this review or anywhere else; an undo takes it away; its title dimmed), – on one skipped here and not followed up today. The card is **the task editor's layout** (task layout B, feedback 2026-11-15, boards B-Editor / B-Review, `task/TaskDetailsBody`), the queue on its left: the box and title — the title a field edited in place as the editor's (`ReviewTitleField`: ⏎ or a blur writes it, one undo step, never blank; Esc puts it back; it wraps and grows, on the medium face one line), then `Created … · Modified …` (gone under 560px) and a ⋯ with the editor header's actions — Open in editor, Duplicate (the copy opens in the editor), Make subtask of…, Delete (a task that goes leaves the live queue, the review moving on) —; the **fact band** (labelled cells in one row on a slightly darker ground, thin dividers between them, wrapping when narrow — each cell's width the band's, never its value's, so a change to one fact moves no other cell and the band wraps by its width alone (E6; Due its date with the repeat under it — typed, `Repeat every [2w]` read back `Every 2 weeks · next Mon, Dec 7` (`task/RepeatField`, schema v84: ⏎ or a blur writes it, one undo step; Esc puts it back; a text that doesn't read says how and isn't written) —, the repeat's lines kept without a date —, so the 920px editor holds all five on one line, People's and Tags' pills wrapping inside their cells); each fact under the standard section label — 11px bold caps, muted, no colour and no icon) — Priority (Focus · Urgent · Important), Status, People and Tags (each a pill with its own ×, then a dashed round **+**, always shown, opening the picker), Due + repeat —; then two columns: on the left Subtasks, then Notes (always a box, one line with its placeholder while empty, growing with its text to 300px and never taking the card's spare room; its toolbar on the NOTES line, shown while Notes has the focus; flushed before any other write), on the right Status updates oldest first, anchored to the bottom, with the review's update line last — every edit in place, each its own undo step, one task only (never the selection). The **footer**: ‹ Back and Skip › at its left end; at its right the follow-up fields — **Last, a field** (a date, never in the future; ⏎ / Tab / blur write it after the notes as its own undo step, ⏎ handing the focus back to the update line; Esc reverts), then Next (the quick intervals, its × clearing it — no None) — and **Mark followed up ›** (⏎ in the update line; its tooltip says what it will do, the field it reads is right beside it), the follow-up popover's rules — the typed update and the follow-up as one undo step — then the next card. In the DOM the fields and Mark followed up come before Back and Skip, so from the update line Tab reaches Last, then (after Last's calendar) Next; as the footer narrows the quick intervals go first, then the fields' relative words, then the labels. Back / Skip write nothing; typed update and Next-date drafts are kept per task within the review. The one **Mark followed up** button (`shared/followup/MarkFollowedUpButton`: follow-up yellow, the flag-check icon, never a changed label) is the editor footer's (after the follow-up fields, before Done; edit mode only), the popover's and the review footer's.

**The task editor** (`TaskEditPopup`, 920px wide) has the same band and columns; its header holds the box, the title, the muted `Created … · Modified …`, ⋯ and ×; its footer `Last · Next [quick] · Mark followed up · Done` (create mode: `Last · Next [quick] · Cancel · Create task`; a draft has no Last); it is **as tall as its content** — the taller column sets it, so few updates make a short popup —, up to 82% of the window, then the columns scroll inside; on a phone one column and the footer wraps, the fields on their own line.

**The Review widget** (item 12; widget feedback 2A, board Review-field; widget kind `review`, under Tasks after Notes; `components/workspace/review/ReviewWidget`): the review in a cell, its rail, card, footer and stepping (`components/review`, over a review session of its own — `stores/review-session.reviewWidgetSession`).

**No kind, no settings**: its config is empty (`ReviewTabConfig`); board BM's kind (`by`), board BK's `pick` and a saved place an earlier build stored, schema v79 removed from every tab (one in a v79 row, a hand edit, is dropped by the layout repair, a fix). What it reviews is **a filter typed into its field** (`ReviewFilterField`): what a list's Expression filter takes (`@person`, `#tag`, `!status`, `/urgent`, date words, `and` / `or` / `not`, parentheses) but `done` and Completed dates (a review takes open tasks only: the field refuses them, saying so), tinted as typed (`hooks/use-expression-field`), and **temporary** — kept by tab id in memory (`list-widget-store.reviewFilters`: the last text that read, as an expression; never layout config, never view state, gone with a reload). A click on the field (a focus while it is empty, the empty state's **Pick**) lists people, tags and statuses under their headings, each with its open tasks under the widget's screen filter, the most counted first; bare letters where a term may go match all three at once (no `@` first), ⏎ taking the first; `@ # ! /` still open the expression popup (`services/review-field.reviewFieldAutocomplete`, drawn by the shared `NlpPopup` with its `mixed` marks, counts and section headings). A pick writes the name's token (`@"Marcus Chen"` quoted as the printer quotes it); after a whole term it joins the filter as chips combine — the names of one sort `or` together, the sorts `and`: Aisha, Ben, then q4 is `(@Aisha or @Ben) and #q4` (`reviewFieldPick` → `withPick`; text, so the words can be changed) —, and after an operator or a parenthesis it goes in as it is. A text that doesn't read shows the expression field's error under the field and is never written: the queue keeps the last filter that read; Esc puts its text back (with nothing to put back, Esc leaves the field); a × clears it (a filter that narrows by nothing — a jump from such a list — reads `Every open task` there, with the ×).

**At rest** (the field not focused, its text the filter's) each term is a pill with its own × (`filter-expression.printPieces`: the printed text in pieces, the joining words muted between the pills): a press on one takes that term out of the filter (`withoutAtom`: with a `not` over it, a group left with one term losing its parentheses; the `done` pill, Show completed), written at once; a press anywhere else goes to the text field under the pills, which shows as typed text again. At the field's end, while the filter names only people (`@A`, `@A or @B` — `peopleOnly`) and only on desktop, **Check-in** (the check-in dialog for those people, the first selected — item 9) and **Paste reply** (the reply import, `openCheckinImport`; it reads the person from the reply). For the Review buttons elsewhere (2B), `services/review-widget` sets a tab's filter and shows it (`setReviewWidgetFilter`, through `services/workspace-tabs.showTab`) and finds the widget a jump sets (`reviewWidgetTarget`: the open layout's first Review tab in reading order; none — the popup review). Alone in its cell its top line reads `Review`, `2 followed up` (with a filter; `All 12 reviewed · 2 followed up` once every task has a mark, at the large face) and the cell's ⋯, the field's row under it; in a cell of several widgets (and in the widget popup) there is no top line — the field's row is its first row and ends with the progress. Its queue is **live** (`hooks/use-review-widget-queue`): the open tasks the filter keeps under the screen filter, in `sortOrder` — a task leaving the filter leaves the rail, the current one stays until the review moves on (`review-queue.keepPlace`). Where it stands — the current task, the – marks — lasts as long as the filter (the tab's session, `filterKey`: a different filter starts a new review, the same one again resumes; cleared with the filter, and with every review's typed text on a data replace), never saved; `N followed up` also counts the tasks followed up in this review that left the queue since; only the rail's sort (**Priority** where the tab stores none, `DEFAULT_REVIEW_WIDGET_SORT`) is view state, saved at once (`data/layout-shape` checks the sort; a place an earlier build saved is ignored and dropped).

**A double-click on a rail row opens the task in its editor** (a single click shows its card). Faces (`utils/widget-face.reviewFaceFor`: small as every widget's; medium while the body is at most 640px wide (the card's two columns) or at most 320px tall; else large, also in a short cell, where both panes scroll: the queue under its sort, and — while the card is under 390px tall, too short for its columns to scroll inside — the card's band and columns as one scroll under its title, each column as tall as it is, its log held to four entries (`Review.module.css`, the `review-card` size container): large — the rail beside the card (board B-Review): the rail beside the card (`ReviewCard`), the footer under both, the card filling its cell, its columns scrolling inside — the queue gives way in a small cell (300px down to 220px), and **under 820px wide the queue folds away** (`utils/widget-face.reviewQueueFolds`), a **Queue** button before the top line's progress opening it as a popover (`ReviewQueueButton`); medium — `ReviewCompactCard` (board BM): the title, a one-line facts summary (Last there; edited through Open card ↗), then the updates dock — the Status updates heading, the latest two entries, the update line (⏎ follows up), the Next row (the field and the quick intervals, which give way in a narrow card) — and the footer with Open card ↗ (the editor); small — `Review · <filter>` (`Review · Pick who or what` with none) and `N left · M followed up`, a click opening the whole widget in the widget popup, its field usable there; no filter — `Pick who or what to review`, `Click the field, or type @person, #tag, !status or any filter. It is temporary and isn’t saved.` and a **Pick** button that focuses the field. Every write is the card's (`useReviewFollowUp`, the card's fields), each its own undo step; a card opening takes the focus only while the user is working in the widget, never from the field's row (the filter is typed there). On a phone it shows its medium face, as the popup review does (its Review buttons open the popup review unless the open layout has a Review widget)
