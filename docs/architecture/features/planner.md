# Planner

**Planner** (2026-11-20; widget kind `planner`, under Tasks after Review, no settings; `components/workspace/planner`): today and the week ahead in one widget — **a scratch pad: a drag only ever changes the plan, never a task's real dates**.

**A task's plan state** is one of three, kept on the task, shown here only and set here or from **Plan for day ›** outside it (`task/plan-menu.planForDayEntries`: every task menu — a list row's, a calendar entry's (the day popover's too), a search result's — and the task editor's and the review card's ⋯; this week's days, read against this week's window, **Unplanned** — any open task joins the Planner there — and, with a plan state, **Clear plan**, read **Remove from Planner** when that takes the task out of it, `utils/planner.plannerClearLeaves` → `services/planner-drop.clearPlannerTask`, the snackbar saying so; inside a Planner its rows have the Planner's own menu instead): none; a **planned day** (`TodoItem.doDate`); **held in Unplanned** (`TodoItem.unplanned`, only `true` stored, schema v86) — never both (`models/todo-item.TaskPlan`). It holds the open tasks the screen filter shows (`useScreenTasks`) that are in Focus, have a plan state, or have a **natural day** — the earlier of the next follow-up and the due date when that is on or before its window's last day, a date before today counting as today, the same day the due date's (`utils/planner.plannerNaturalDay`, `inPlanner`).

**Where a task sits** (`plannerPlacement`): held → the **Unplanned** tray; a planned day before today → the **Delayed** tray (nothing joins Today on its own), inside the window → that day, today included; past the window's last day (**planned later**) → its natural day when it has one — a real date inside the window, or overdue, still shows the task: a placed row there —, else not shown in this window; no plan → its natural day; no plan and no natural day → a Focus task is in Unplanned, anything else is not in the widget. A row is **placed** when it has a plan state.

**The planned day** is the `doDate`'s — unless that is the very day of the task's earliest real date, wherever it lies: such a plan is **redundant** and reads as no plan (`plannerPlannedDay`: not placed, no plan mark, and once the day has passed the task is overdue in Today, never in Delayed — read here, so it holds whether the plan or an edit of the date came last: Add task typed with a due date of today, a due date edited onto the planned day). A row on its planned day — and one planned later — carries its **conflicts** (`PlannerConflict`): each real date the plan falls after — the due date, the follow-up —, by how many days, with the fixes on offer: move that date to the planned day (always), or plan for that date's day (only when it is today or later).

**The window** is seven days from today plus a whole number of weeks (never before this week); it is the tab's in its layout, in memory (`stores/planner-store`, by layout id and tab id — so the widget popup's remount keeps it while a duplicated layout, which keeps its tabs' ids, starts on this week; a tab that stops being a Planner loses it, `use-planner.usePlannerWindowsOf`; gone with a reload). `plannerView` gives **Today** — the tasks planned for today and those whose natural day is today, a late date among them, whatever the window —, a group per day ahead, the empty ones too, from the window's first day (never before tomorrow) to its last, the two trays — **Delayed** and **Unplanned** —, and the window's seven **tiles**: each the rows sitting on its day (`count`), the real dates falling on it (`dates`: one mark per due date and per follow-up of every open task, wherever it is planned or held; a late one counts on today's tile) and the plans on it (`plans`: the tasks whose planned day it is) — drawn as the tile's marks (`plannerTileMarks`): a **solid tick** per real date, due dates in the deadline color then follow-ups in the follow-up color, then a **hollow tick** (outlined, in the secondary text color) per plan, eight in all, then `+N`. While the window is shifted, a task dated or planned between tomorrow and the window is in no group; the trays stay. Inside a group the tasks follow the tab's **hand order** (`PlannerTabViewState.order`: task `uid`s, one sequence for the widget, view state — written by a drop into a group, below; the layout repair keeps its strings once each, 1000 at most), the rest after them by reason (due, follow-up, plan, held, Focus), then date, then `sortOrder` (`plannerOrder`).

**Faces** (`utils/widget-face.plannerFaceFor`): large from 720 × 300px — the strip (`PlannerStrip`: `‹`, seven tiles of one width, weekends too, `›`; a thin rule in a slightly wider gap stands before the tile that starts a week — by Settings' first day of the week, `settings-store.weekStartsOn`, the calendar's; never before the first tile —; **a tile's click scrolls the day list to its day** — smoothly, at once under reduced motion — and picks nothing; a tile's name says its marks (`Mon, Nov 23: 2 tasks, 1 due, 1 planned`); the arrows move the window a week, `‹` off on this week), then two panes: on the left **one scrolling list of days** — Today, then every later day of the window in order, a day with nothing on it a quiet heading (its name muted, no count) that still takes a drop —, and on the right, on a darker ground (`--color-inset-deep`), the trays in a scroll of their own — **Delayed** (only while it holds a task), the **↓ Unplan** button centered on a hairline rule (with Delayed; named `Unplan all Delayed tasks`, its tooltip `Move all Delayed tasks to Unplanned`) and **Unplanned** (always there; `Nothing unplanned` while empty) —; medium in a **tall, narrow cell** (`utils/widget-face.plannerPortrait`: under 720px wide, at least 560px tall and 1.4 × as tall as wide; not the phone; board R1, 2026-11-21) — **the portrait layout**: the strip over the days in one scroll, and under them **the trays dock** (`planner/PlannerDock`: Delayed, the Unplan button, Unplanned on the darker ground in a scroll of their own; its top edge a separator sharing the height — dragged, ↑ / ↓ 16px, a double-click resetting it — saved as the tab's view state `dockShare`, default 0.38; its chevron folds it, `dockFolded`, to one bar — `Delayed 2 · Unplanned 6` — that unfolds on a click and is a drop target meaning Unplanned); alone in its cell the week arrows sit in the top line around the range, the narrow tiles keep their ticks, and a 2px line under the tiles marks the days whose groups show in the day list (`planner-dock.useDaysInView`) —; medium otherwise (narrower or shorter, not tall; the phone) — the strip over one column in that order: Today, the days ahead, Delayed, the Unplan button, Unplanned in one scroll —; small — its name and `Today N`, a click opening it larger. A strip under 520px stacks each tile's weekday, date and count and draws no ticks; a widget under 300px wide has the two arrows around the range in words instead of tiles. Alone in its cell its top line reads `Planner`, the window's range (`Nov 20 – 26`; shifted, a button back to this week) and a badge capsule: what it holds that is due and due for a follow-up in Settings' tab-badge ranges — and, at its end, **Reset plan** (an icon and the words, the icon alone under 420px): a menu of This week (the shown range while the window is shifted) · Today only · Delayed, each with the count of tasks it would clear and inert at 0. A cell of tabs and the widget popup have no top line, so the same three are under **Reset plan ›** in the widget's ⋯ menu (`planner/planner-reset`: `usePlannerResetEntries`, and `usePlannerMenuEntries` through `cell-menu.useWidgetMenuContent`). Both read what the drawn Planner reports (`planner-store.resets`, written by `hooks/use-planner` from the view it already holds: the counts, the first choice's name and the action), so the view is built once per change and a cell with no Planner reads nothing; a Planner that isn't drawn (a tab behind another) offers none.

**Rows** are the app's task rows as a list draws them — the list's draggable row (`list/ListWidgetRow`: the tab's id its scope, the group's key its section; `MobileTaskRow` on the phone) at Settings' Default list zoom (Auto by each pane's width: Stacked under 480px), one `TaskRowGrid` per pane, under headers in the list's group-header look (name, count, a rule; no count on an empty day) —; a click opens the task; there is no selection or bulk bar, and it is no list widget (`LIST_WIDGET_ATTR` is absent: the list zoom never finds it).

**A row carries four Planner-only things** (`planner/PlannerRowExtras`; the parts inside the row through `task/task-row-extras.TaskRowExtrasContext` — `(todo) => { tag?, actions?, late? }`, read by `TaskRow`, `TaskLine` and the phone row's details; null without a provider, so a list's rows, the task dialog's and the drag overlay's copy draw exactly as they did): a **corner flag** on every placed row — a 9px filled triangle at the row's top-left in the secondary text color, taking no press, drawn by the Planner around the row (`PlannerRowWrap`), so it shows over the hover fill, over the slot a lifted row leaves, at every zoom and on the phone; drawn only, its words in the row's accessible description (`planned`, `planned for Wed` on a Delayed row, `set aside`: `plannerRowStateWords` → `TaskRowExtras.description`, on the row's checkbox, a line, the phone row's button) —; a **tag** after the title (`utils/planner.plannerRowTag`; a Stacked row's leads its details line, the phone's is on a line under the row): a Delayed row's planned day in words (`Wed`; past six days `Nov 12`), muted; a held row's natural day, muted; on a row planned past a real date `1 day after due` / `2 days after follow-up` (the due date's when both are passed) in the deadline color, and on a row planned later `planned Fri, Nov 27`, muted, its date drawn late too — each a button whose menu lists that row's fixes (`plannerFixLabel`: `Move due date to Tue, Nov 24`, `Plan for Mon, Nov 23`; Esc hands the focus back to the tag) —; the date the plan falls after **drawn late** (bold over its type's faint band) though it may not be past — the look only, its name and tooltip unchanged —; and **Snooze** and **Unplan** text buttons at the title's end on the row's hover and focus (in the row's hover cluster; none on a screen with no hover, on a Lines row or on the phone) — a row in Unplanned has neither, nothing there being planned to put off: it has **Today**, the drop on today (`planner-drop.planPlannerTaskToday`), and — held there, flagged — **Remove** (its plan cleared, out of the Planner) or, when a date in the window or Focus keeps it there, **Clear** (`clearPlannerTask`). Pressed from the keyboard — the buttons, a fix from a menu opened with Enter — **the focus follows the row** (`planner/planner-focus`): to the row at its new place, the same control when it has it; when the row left the view, to the row that took its place, else to its group's heading.

**Drops** (desktop; the phone has no drag): every day tile and every group — Today (today), each day ahead (an empty day's heading too), the two trays — is a target for a dragged task: a row of this Planner, a list widget's row, a calendar entry. Each is a dnd-kit droppable (`TASK_DROP_KIND.plannerDay` / `.plannerGroup`, its drop data the Planner, the day, the window and — a group — its rows), so a clipped or covered one never counts (`task-dnd/collision`), and the page's dispatcher makes the drop, once (`dispatchTaskDrop` → `services/planner-drop`; the widget's own drag hook writes nothing, and a list's `useListRowDrag` only takes its own tab's rows inside its own scroller).

**The write is `utils/planner.plannerDrop`'s decision** — the only place the drop rule lives — for the task in that Planner's window, with no chooser: on a day `T` (a tile, a day's group, Today = today) — `T` the task's natural day → whatever plan state it has is cleared (`own`: a redundant plan for that very day too, and a later plan; with none to clear, nothing), otherwise — unless it sits there already — it is planned for `T` (`plan`: `doDate = T`, `unplanned` cleared); on **Unplanned** — a task with a natural day is **held** (`unplanned`, `doDate` cleared); a planned one without: in Focus it just loses its plan (Focus keeps it there), else it is held too — **Unplan never takes a task out of the Planner** —; any other open task is held too — Unplanned is how a task with no date joins the Planner, flagged —; **Delayed takes no drop** (a row already in it only reorders); where the row sits already, nothing (the order only); a completed task is refused everywhere.

**No due date and no follow-up is ever written**: each drop is one task write of both plan fields and one undo step (`todo-store.setPlan`), which never stamps `modifiedAt` and logs no history event.

**Snooze** plans the task for the day after the day it sits on — after its planned day, for a row planned later —, never earlier than tomorrow (a Delayed row: tomorrow; a row in Unplanned offers no Snooze), and **Unplan** is the Unplanned drop for one row — both through the same decision, so a snoozed task landing on its natural day loses its plan (`plannerSnooze`, `plannerUnplan`).

**Nothing leaves the Planner silently**: a Snooze from the window's last day keeps a task with a real date in the window where that date puts it, planned later; one with no such date goes out of view (the strip's `›` shows it), and the move that took it there — Snooze, a drop, Plan for day › — shows the snackbar naming the day, with Undo (`Planned for Fri, Nov 27`; `plannerMovePlan`'s `hidden`, from `plannerShows`), as does a fix that moves a date past the window (`Moved the due date to Fri, Nov 27`); **Unplan all Delayed** moves every Delayed task to Unplanned and **Reset plan** clears the plan state of the days shown (every task with a plan state that the Planner shows or holds: planned days it shows, Delayed, held in Unplanned — named `This week` only while the window starts today, else by its range, `Nov 27 – Dec 3`: `plannerResetWeekLabel`), of today only (the tasks planned for today) or of Delayed — each **after asking** (the shared confirm prompt: how many plans it clears, and how many of those tasks then leave the Planner) —, each one write and one undo step with the snackbar's Undo (`plannerUnplanDelayed`, `plannerResetTasks`, `plannerResetCounts` → `services/planner-drop.unplanDelayedTasks`, `resetPlannerPlan`); a conflict's fix **Move due date / follow-up to the planned day** is the one Planner write that touches a real date — the ordinary task edit, the day at local midnight: stamped, undoable, in the history — and when the task's own dates then put it on its planned day, the plan is cleared in the same edit (one undo step), so the row is no longer placed; with another, earlier date still putting it elsewhere the plan stays (`plannerFixClearsPlan`, `applyPlannerFix`).

**Dropped into a group the row also lands where it was dropped** (and a row dragged inside its group is reordered, with no task write): the row's new place is **merged into the tab's stored `order`** (`plannerReorder`, through `workspace-store.patchTabViewState`: saved at once, no undo step, as a list's manual order): the moved row goes beside its visible neighbours, every task shown takes a place (one the order didn't hold right after the row shown before it), and every other stored task keeps its position relative to every other — so a task the Planner doesn't show just now (hidden by the screen filter, on a day a shifted window leaves out, completed) keeps its place; only a task the todo store no longer holds (deleted, purged) leaves the order; at most 1000, the tasks not shown going first, the earliest first; a drop on a tile keeps the order. A Planner row dropped on a Calendar widget's day is the calendar's drop, as any list row's; on a list it does nothing.

**While a task is dragged over the Planner** (`use-planner-drag` → `planner-store.drag`, from dnd-kit's `over` and the pointer over the groups as measured when the drag started): the target's tile and group are dashed in one neutral color — no real date changes — (`data-planner-drop`), the list's blank drop slot shows where the row would land (the dragged row its slot in its own group, faded in place when headed elsewhere, the others dimmed while they make room — the list's looks), and **the lifted row carries a label** under it — above it at the window's foot — saying what the drop does to the plan: `Plan · Wed, Nov 25`, `Unplan`, or — the plan cleared on the task's own date's day — `Its date · Wed, Nov 25` (`PlannerDragLabel`, in the page's drag overlay); a refused target has no mark, no slot and no label, and shows the not-allowed cursor; a drop that moves the row to another group lands where it was let go, a reorder settles onto the row.

**Plan for day ›, Snooze, Unplan** (`planner-menu`, a row's task menu in a Planner — right-click, Shift+F10, the phone's long-press — through `TaskMenuExtrasContext`): the same moves without a pointer — Plan for day ›'s submenu a muted first line (`Plans it for that day`), then Today and the window's days in words (the day the row sits on checked), a pick being the drop on that day; Snooze, naming the day it plans for (none on a row in Unplanned); Unplan, off with its reason in a tooltip for a row in Unplanned already; and, on a placed row, Clear plan (Remove from Planner when that takes it out). A row completed here lingers, then folds, as a list's (`hooks/use-planner` over `useLeavingRows`); under Today's rows **`N done`** lists the tasks completed today (the tally's count, `utils/tally.tasksDoneToday`), struck, on one line; Today has no Add task row. On the phone a day with no tasks and an empty Unplanned are left out (a tile's tap goes to the next day that has a group), and a completed row leaves at once

**One write stamps nothing: a task's plan state in the Planner** — its planned day (`TodoItem.doDate`, schema v85: the day the user plans to work on the task, a day at local midnight) or held in Unplanned (`TodoItem.unplanned`, schema v86: only `true` stored), never both; neither indexed; the Planner's, shown nowhere else and set only there, from **Plan for day ›** in every task menu — a row's, a calendar entry's, a search result's — and in the task editor's and the review card's ⋯ (`task/plan-menu`): no filter, expression, sort, row column, calendar, Quick Add, Markdown export or check-in reads them): planning is not an edit, so `todo-store.setPlan(ids, plan)` / `setPlans([{ id, plan }])` — `plan` a `TaskPlan`: `{ day }`, `'unplanned'` or `null`; **both fields in the task's one write** (`store-helpers.bulkUpdateTasks`), one undo step for all the tasks, with the snackbar only where the caller asks (the Planner's bulk actions: Reset plan, Unplan all Delayed), `Planned “Title”` / `Unplanned “Title”` / `Cleared the plan of “Title”` / `Changed the plan of 3 tasks` or the caller's words — leaves every task's `modifiedAt` as it is on the write, its undo and its redo (which put both fields back), in the store and the database (`utils/task-stamps.KEEP_STAMPS`, a stamp that lists no task), logs no history event and never writes a due date or a follow-up. It never rejects: a write that fails is taken back — a task's two plan fields together, and only while both still hold that write's values, so overlapping failures can't leave a planned day beside `unplanned` (`bulkUpdateTasks`' `together`) —, the tasks are read back, the user is told (`Couldn't save the plan of “Title”.`) and it resolves `false`. Completing a repeating task clears the plan state on the next occurrence (the checkbox and the bulk path — a check-in reply's too —, the undo putting it back); Duplicate doesn't copy it; Make subtask of… drops it without naming it; exports, backups, the sync file, task archives and the append import carry it (a file holding both keeps the planned day). Destructive operations — a data replace, a purge, a bulk delete of more than five tasks, removing unused people and tags — back up first (`takeSafetySnapshot`; a failed backup asks before going on). New tasks come through `QuickAddBar` (its Details hands off to `TaskEditPopup`'s create mode).
