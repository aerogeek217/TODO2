# Task maps

**Each zone's word carries its count and opens its tasks as a list** (the task dialog, `glance-drills.neglectZoneDrill`; a zone with none is plain words): the four corners' words, and the strip's No due date (every task without one) and Forgotten. The numbered Slipping list sits beside the plot only while the widget is at least 900px wide (`NEGLECT_SIDE_LIST_MIN_PX`; under the plot on a phone); narrower, the plot fills the widget, there is no list and the dots carry no numbers. Its config (layout config, no schema step: Color by the picked tags — a new map's are the `Project: …` tags — / People / None, and the threshold) is set in its ⋯; a dot opens the editor, a drag on empty plot lassoes dots into the widget selection the bulk bar acts on.

**A task's name stands beside its dot where it fits** (`utils/neglect-labels.placeNeglectLabels`, over the dots as drawn and each name's measured width; the boxes in a grid, so a name looks only at those near it, and the work capped — `NEGLECT_LABEL_WORK_MAX`): right of the dot, else left, clear of every dot, badge, other name and the plot's own words — the Slipping tasks first, then the flagged ones —, cut with an ellipsis where there is less room than the name (at most 160px), absent under 56px; the names take no press, so a bigger map simply names more of its tasks.

**The task maps** (2026-11-18, `features/task-maps-2026-11-18`; layout config, no schema step; faces by `utils/widget-face.taskMapFaceFor`): **Work clusters** (kind `clusters`, `components/workspace/clusters/`, no settings) draws the open tasks' people and tags as bubbles sized by their tasks, a person joined to a tag by the tasks they share; clusters found by label propagation, outlined and named by their biggest tag (its name opens its tasks), bridges between them in amber, a bubble opening the person / tag menu (`utils/work-clusters`, a deterministic force layout). The **List wall** (kind `listwall`, `components/workspace/listwall/`, no settings; `hooks/use-list-wall`) shows every list tab of the layout at once: a block per list, a square per open task it shows (as its tab would: its filter, prompt pick and the screen filter), the trouble first — overdue, due soon, follow-up late, on track, stale: its fill —, its priority a ring and Focus's glow as on the task's checkbox (`PriorityRing`); the blocks sit side by side, as many as fit at 8 squares across, sharing the width (`list-wall.wallShape`: a wide cell shows every list in one row, never wrapping into empty width; 2–6 rows, 12 in one row of blocks); the name's tooltip is the legend (Work clusters' likewise — no ⓘ on a widget); a list's name brings its tab to the front of its cell, a square opens its task; in a narrow or short cell a bar per list (`listWallFaceFor`).
