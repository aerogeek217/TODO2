# The screen filter

**The screen filter** is the top bar's chips and the expression typed beside them (`FilterCriteria.expression`, ANDed with the chips), one for every layout and never saved (`filter-store`; read by every widget through `stores/screen-filter`): wherever it applies — lists, the glance widgets, the Neglect map, the calendar, the filtered-tasks panel and its counts — a task must pass it. (Lenses — named screen filters picked in the top bar, with a switch on every widget — were removed, user feedback 2026-11-15; schema v81 deleted their data.) Clear all (the field's × at its right end, or the Filter menu's last item) clears the chips and the expression; the tray turns red while one applies. **The funnel pauses it** (feedback 2026-11-15, `filter-store.paused`, in memory only): the chips, the expression, the text and Show completed stay, but nothing applies them — `stores/screen-filter` (its hooks and `currentScreenFilter`) gives no filter, so every list, glance widget, the calendar, the Neglect map, the counts and the filtered-tasks panel show every task —; the tray loses its red, the pills dim and are struck through, the counter reads `109 open · paused` (the phone's pill too, a tap resuming); a click again resumes it, and so does any edit of the filter (typing it, a pill's ×, a Filter menu pick — every write goes through `commit`), Clear all and a reload end it; the palette's **Pause / Resume Filter** and the editable `pause-filter` shortcut (no keys by default) toggle it. The screen-filter hooks hand the widgets the filter deferred (`useDeferredValue`), so a clear, pause or resume paints the top bar in the click's frame and the widgets follow. The chips are set from the top bar's one **Filter** button (UX cleanup, decision 4: its menu lists Priority, Tags, People, Dates and Status — each opening its picker beside the menu —, then Completed and, while any filter is on, Clear all filters; `F` opens it; its name counts what is on) and from the field. **The typed filter** (feedback P2, board Filter-B; `components/layout/TopBarFilterField`, placeholder `@person #tag due fri`): the field takes the list Expression grammar (item 6) and the search box's forms (`parseExpression`'s `searchForms`: terms side by side are an `and`, but side-by-side names of one kind or priorities an `or` — `@a @b #q4` is `(@a or @b) and #q4` —; `completed` alone is `done`; `/due ⟨day⟩`, `/followup ⟨day | none⟩`), tinted as typed, with the `@ # ! /` popup (`useExpressionField`, its input a one-line box). A text that reads is applied once typing rests, on ⏎ and on blur (`filter-store.applyExpression`): each top-level `and` term a chip can say becomes that chip (`splitTopLevel`, one per field, one Priority chip — the pickers then show it), the other terms are held as the screen filter's `expression`; a text that doesn't read is never applied — its error under the field (not while the popup shows), the tasks keeping the last filter that read —, and Esc puts the filter's text back (with nothing to put back, Esc leaves the field; over the filtered-tasks panel the panel takes it). **At rest** each term is a pill with its own × (`shared/filters/ExpressionPills`, the Review widget field's: `printPieces`, `withoutAtom`), joining words and brackets muted between; too long, they fade at the field's end with `+N more` (the top-level terms not shown) and the tooltip holds the whole filter. A press anywhere else in the field shows the whole filter as text — the chips printed as their terms, in the order typed while the filter is still as the field wrote it (`filter-store.fieldFilter`) —, and what is written there is split again. The text search's words list their matches under the field (`TopBarSearchResults`) while the popup is closed. The phone keeps its chip sheet and its own field (`services/search-tokens` → `filter-store.applySearchTokens`: each token a chip as it completes, Backspace in the empty box taking the last back); a typed expression shows there read-only, in words, with a × that clears it (`shared/filters/ScreenExpression`). The counter `61 of 109` says what the chips keep of every open task — and, while they let completed tasks through, of the completed ones they admit (Show completed: every one; a chips' Completed range: the ones in it), so `of N` never grows with the whole history.
